use four trails on small screens. They do not change any application state.

## Generation prompt

Create a single transparent PNG decorative foliage asset for the TOP LEFT CORNER of this website, using the attached screenshot's upper-corner leaves as the close stylistic reference. This is an asset extraction/recreation, NOT a website mockup. Output ONLY one hanging leafy branch isolated on a genuinely transparent background, absolutely no UI, letters, stars, border, grid, white rectangle, scenery or other objects.

A delicate diagonal brown woody branch enters at the top-left edge and extends a short distance rightward across the top and downward along the left corner, making a natural loose inverted L silhouette. About 12-16 distinct tapered oval / pointed lance-shaped green leaves cluster along small fine twigs. Leaves must be unmistakably botanical: tapered ends, fuller gently rounded middles, visible central veins with occasional small lateral veins, distinct sunny highlights and darker forest-green underside shading. Match the charming detailed 16-bit pixel-art leaves in the screenshot, with small square pixels at the contour, dark forest outline one or two fine pixels, layered moss-green, fresh green and yellow-green shades. Avoid coarse cross/plus/block/arrow shapes; avoid bare zigzag square sticks. A leafy branch, not a geometric diagram.

Composition: approximately square 640x640 canvas, foliage confined to the top-left approximately 80% of the canvas in an airy corner arc, generous transparent negative space in the lower right. Branch naturally clipped at top and left image edges, with complete leaves elsewhere, no framed square silhouette. Pixel detail should remain appealing and readable when the entire asset is displayed around 180-220 CSS pixels wide. Light, organic, restrained, clean edges and real alpha transparency. No blur or cast shadow, no text or watermark.
